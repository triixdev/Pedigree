What it is

Pedigree compares the license a model declares with the license of the model it was made from, and points out differences. It is a research aid.

It is not legal advice

A flag means a person should take a look. It is not a finding that anyone broke a license, and publishers may have reasons that Pedigree cannot see. Only the Llama 3 and Llama 3.2 rules have been checked against the license text. Every other rule is marked unverified. Read the real license text and ask a qualified person before you act on anything shown here.

Information about models

Model information comes from Hugging Face and belongs to the people who published it. It can be wrong, out of date or missing. Pedigree does not change it.

How you may use it

  • Do not use it to harass, accuse or pressure the people who publish models.
  • Do not try to overload it, break it, or read other people's saved decisions.
